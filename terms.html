Membership & registration

Season registration is free. Members are responsible for their own postage and for complying with national postal regulations in their country of origin.

  • Registration is completed via Wix Events for the active season mail-off or designated registration event.
  • You must provide accurate contact information. False identities may result in disqualification.
  • One league account per person. Shared accounts are not permitted.
02

Competition

All races are governed by the official Race Rules. The league's decisions on eligibility, transit times, and standings are final, though we welcome polite correspondence.

  • Arrival dates are verified only via league-operated PO box receipt and postmark photography.
  • Cards declared lost after five years receive honourable mention but do not count as a finish.
  • The league accepts no liability for cards lost, delayed, or nibbled by wildlife en route.
