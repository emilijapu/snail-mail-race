# Snail Mail Racing League — Creative Brief

**HEADLESS DAY brief spec-0324** · Wild Card · medium

> Competitive postal racing: slowest delivery wins.

A global league where members mail identical postcards through the world's slowest routes, and the last to arrive wins.

## Requirements

- Current season standings table
- Rules page explaining race formats
- Hall of fame page with legendary slow deliveries
- League registration form
- Mobile-first responsive design
- **Bonus:** live "days in transit" counter for race leaders

## Art direction

| | |

## Audience & voice

**Audience.** Mail-art hobbyists, geography nerds, irony-loving adults 25–55.

**Voice.** deadpan · bureaucratic-absurd · patient · internationally formal · dry-witted

**Avoid.** corporate buzzwords, urgency language, speed metaphors
