Form 14-A · Procedure

How a race works

Three steps. No tracking numbers, no expedited anything. The only performance metric is patience.

1

Choose a slow corridor

Pick an origin and a route through at least one approved slow corridor. Direct airmail between capital cities is, regrettably, disqualified.

2

Mail the league postcard

Send the identical league-stamped postcard to our Zurich PO box. Postage is on you — usually under a dollar, occasionally twelve.

3

Arrive last, win the season

The final postcard to reach the PO box wins. Our postmaster photographs and timestamps each arrival. That's the whole sport.

The Rule Book · Ratified Season 1

Race rules & formats

The official statutes governing eligible routes, postcard specifications, and grounds for disqualification. Read in full before your first entry.

Document SMRL-RB-14 · Rev. 7 Zurich · Ratified 12 Sept 2012 · Amended annually
01

Race formats

Three sanctioned formats run each season. All share one victory condition: the last card to arrive wins.

  • Open Corridor — free choice of destination; any approved slow route qualifies. The classic format.
  • Fixed Terminus — every racer mails to the same shared destination. Origin is your only variable.
  • Return to Sender — mail a card to yourself the long way round. Purists' favourite; historically the slowest.
02

Eligible routes

A route must traverse at least one corridor from the current approved list. Speed is a foul, not a feature.

  • Surface mail (sea and land) is strongly encouraged; airmail permitted only on legs with no surface option.
  • Direct capital-to-capital airmail is prohibited and voids the entry.
  • Deliberate misrouting through remote sorting facilities is entirely within the spirit of the league.
03

Postcard specification

Every entry uses the identical league-issued postcard so that no card gains an unfair advantage in handling.

  • Standard 105 × 148 mm league stock, 300 gsm, matte. No lamination, no rigid backing.
  • Must bear the league seal and your member handle on the reverse.
  • One message permitted. Weatherproofing is at your peril; the elements are part of the sport.
04

Disqualification

We adjudicate slowly and reluctantly. The following, however, will end a run.

  • Any use of courier, express, or tracked services on any leg.
  • Tampering with postmarks, or paying a clerk to hold your card. We will find out. We have time.
  • Cards declared lost after five years earn an honourable mention but do not count as a finish.
