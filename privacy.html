Privacy policy

Effective 1 January 2025 · Snail Mail Racing League, Bahnhofstrasse 12, 8001 Zurich, Switzerland

Document SMRL-PP-01 · Rev. 3 Controller: Snail Mail Racing League · league@snailmailracing.org
01

What we collect

We collect only what is needed to run a global postal racing league with a straight face.

  • Season registration — name, email, country, and preferred race format when you register via Wix Events.
  • General inquiries — name, email, and message when you contact league HQ.
  • Member accounts — login email, profile details, and race entries you log in your account area.
  • Technical data — standard server logs (IP address, browser type) required to operate the site securely.
02

How we use it

Your data is used to administer races, reply to correspondence, and keep the standings honest.

  • Process season registrations and event RSVPs.
  • Respond to inquiries submitted through the contact form.
  • Display your member race history to you when logged in.
  • Send operational messages about your entry — not marketing blasts unless you opt in.
03

Third parties

This site is powered by Wix Headless. Registration, forms, member login, and CMS data are processed on Wix infrastructure subject to Wix's privacy policy. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

04

Retention & your rights

We retain registration and inquiry records for as long as needed to administer the league and meet legal obligations. Under Swiss and applicable EU data protection law you may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by writing to league@snailmailracing.org. We will respond within a reasonable period — by league standards, promptly.
